Why CAP Exists

CAP was created from a simple idea: greater awareness can encourage people to pay attention to changes in their health and seek appropriate professional advice sooner. The project focuses on education, awareness and access to reliable health information.

Important Health Information

CAP provides educational information and is not a diagnostic tool or a replacement for a doctor, nurse, dentist or other qualified healthcare professional. A change mentioned on this website does not automatically mean cancer. Many changes have other causes. If you notice a new, unusual or persistent change that concerns you, seek advice from an appropriate healthcare professional.
